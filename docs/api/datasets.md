# Datasets

Small demo datasets used by the tutorials. The Caquetá bundle (about 40 MB) holds NISAR PROVISIONAL HV backscatter and HH coherence over a deforestation frontier in Colombia, with RADD alerts, Sentinel-2 imagery and clearing outlines on one 20 m grid. It downloads once and is cached in `~/.cache/nice-sar` (or `$NICE_SAR_DATA`).
